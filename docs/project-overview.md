# GlintPM Mobile App — Project Overview

## 1. Product vision
Build a reliable mobile channel through which GlintPM learners and customers can access professional training, career opportunities, practical resources, and technical support.

The product should support GlintPM's positioning around project planning, scheduling, project controls, professional development, and technology-enabled solutions.

Brand direction:
- Primary green: `#085634`
- Gold accent: `#F3BA25`
- Cyan accent: `#1EC9DF`
- Brand line: “Plan better, control better, deliver better.”

## 2. Target users
- Prospective learners exploring GlintPM training.
- Enrolled students accessing course information and learning materials.
- Professionals seeking planning, scheduling, and project controls resources.
- Job seekers looking for relevant career opportunities.
- Customers requesting technical job support or consulting.
- GlintPM administrators managing content and customer activity.

## 3. Main product areas
1. **Home Dashboard:** personalized summaries, announcements, and shortcuts.
2. **Training Academy:** courses, tracks, cohorts, sessions, enrollment, and progress.
3. **Career Hub:** job listings, search, filters, and saved jobs.
4. **Professional Resources:** articles, templates, documents, and other learning resources.
5. **Job Support & Consulting:** structured enquiry submission and enquiry management.

## 4. MVP goals
- Provide a clear mobile experience for the five main product areas.
- Allow users to register and securely access their accounts.
- Present accurate training and cohort information.
- Support course enrollment and verified payments.
- Allow users to discover career opportunities and save jobs.
- Provide controlled access to resources.
- Capture and manage support enquiries.
- Give administrators a practical way to manage operational content.

## 5. Out of scope for the initial MVP
Unless separately approved, the initial MVP does not include:
- Primavera P6 XER parsing or schedule analysis.
- DCMA 14-point schedule assessment.
- Automated schedule risk analysis.
- Project cost or payment assurance workflows.
- Full project controls command center.
- AI-powered schedule review.
- Full project management workspace.

These may be considered in later product versions.

## 6. Success measures
Define measurable targets before launch. Candidate measures include:
- Account registration completion.
- Course detail views and enrollment conversion.
- Successful payment-to-enrollment conversion.
- Active learners and course progress.
- Job saves and outbound application clicks.
- Support enquiry completion and response time.
- App crash rate and API error rate.

Do not invent baseline values or targets. Establish them using GlintPM business data and product priorities.

## 7. Assumptions requiring confirmation
- Final payment provider and supported currencies.
- Final production hosting provider.
- Object storage provider for private course resources.
- Whether job application tracking belongs in the MVP.
- Whether push notifications are required for the first release.
- Final privacy policy, terms, and data-retention requirements.
